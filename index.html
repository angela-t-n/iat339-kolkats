Interactive Elements

In-text link

Sample code:

<p>Help <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat">Kolkat</a> save the [plant name] forest before it is too late! When you purchase from the Plantosphere, you will be appointed an endangered plant. But not just any plant. This plant will grant you your deepest desires.</p>
