What is Plantoscope?

Plantoscope (derived from “plant” and “horoscope”) provides fortune readings in the form of blind box purchases containing a plant and fortune scroll. Plantoscope offers different readings from each region of Mystic Forest! Be it love, luck, health, or personal identity, each set provides a different variety of plants pertaining to your selected category.
